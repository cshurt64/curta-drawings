Compared with 10.102

Same outside diameter (Ø19) and bore fit (Ø16 H9) as 10.102, but this ring is stepped: a 7 mm-wide inner recess 1 mm deep on one face, giving an overall thickness of 2 mm against 10.102's flat 1.1 mm. Two of these sit in the tens bell against 10.102's one.
