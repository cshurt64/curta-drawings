The same sleeve carries 10.051 (1.9 mm), 10.054 (1.3 mm) and, per the 10.220 assembly, the ratchet wheel 10.049 (0.5 mm) and the drive wheel 10.053 (0.6 ±0.020 mm, read from its own sheet 10.053-3, Part-Dimensions-10053.md). Adding 1.9 + 1.3 + 0.5 + 0.6 = 4.3 mm against the 4.8 mm reduced length leaves 0.5 mm unaccounted for, so the stack does not close on the sheets (Unverified; it may include a washer or free play). 10.051 and 10.054 have Ø2.3 H8 bores, which on this h7 diameter gives 0 to 23 µm of clearance (Derived from the sheets' limits; the pairing is Inferred by nominal size).

The annealing note (½ Stunde geglüht, here geglüht, half an hour at 300–320° on the twin sheet 10.140) applies to the brass; the flange bore Ø2 H8 is marked * (special inspection).

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size.
