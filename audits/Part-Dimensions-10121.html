Bore Ø2.5 H8 (over 1–3 mm) IT8 = 14 µm; EI = 0 2.500–2.514 mm +14/0 Confirmed; agrees

The stem disagreement is larger than the 1 µm shifts seen on F/f/N/R fits elsewhere in this set, and its width (9 µm) matches neither the 3–6 mm IT7 (12 µm) nor the 1–3 mm IT7 (10 µm). The cause is Unverified (a pre-ISO table or a different size step are the obvious candidates, neither confirmed). Against the pawl's H8 bore (+18/0) the sheet values give roughly −9 µm (clearance) to +18 µm (interference); ISO's p7 would give −6 to +24 µm.
