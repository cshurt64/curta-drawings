The grading rule. The note Von 1/100 zu 1/100 nach untenstehender Tabelle aussortieren u. kennzeichnen (sort in 0.01 mm steps by the table below and mark) and the table sort the posts by the 36.75 ±20 dimension into four grades, marked by groove count:

Position Length between shoulders (mm) Grooves
1 36.73–36.74 1
2 over 36.74–36.75 2
3 over 36.75–36.76 3
4 over 36.76–36.77 4

This is a selective fit: the parent 10.232 drawing pairs 10.231 position 1 with 10.064 position 1 or 2 and position 2 with position 3 or 4, and says all posts in one assembly carry the same groove count (Sämtliche Säulen mit gleicher Rillenzahl). The four grades together cover the whole 36.73–36.77 mm toleranced range.
