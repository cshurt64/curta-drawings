At 1× nominal, three 10.038 discs plus two 10.042 spacers and the 10.041 sleeve total 3 × 0.5 + 2 × 1.0 + 1.8 = 5.3 mm against the 10.020 sleeve's 5.8 mm reduced length, leaving 0.5 mm nominal unallocated (Derived). The drawing gives no individual gaps, so the stack cannot be closed to a controlled axial fit. The 10.038 pre-broaching bore limits (2.255–2.269 mm) appear smaller than the 10.020 Ø2.3 h7 shaft. The sleeve nevertheless specifies a tight sliding fit and the assembly calls for finish broaching; the final bore/shaft clearance after that operation is Unverified. No assembly fit is inferred solely from the pre-broaching dimensions. Radially, each 10.038 disc reaches Ø4.4–4.45 mm; the assembly sheet gives no mating diameter or clearance. The 105° marks show angular positions, but the exact clocking datum between the three discs is Unverified.

Project 3× reference

Per the project's tolerance strategy and project decisions, fabrication tolerances are chosen for the process. Simply tripling a tolerance is a mathematical comparison only; general and non-ISO tolerances do not carry over silently. If the project applies an ISO grade, it must be recalculated at the scaled basic size.
