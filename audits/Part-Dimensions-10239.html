Angular tolerance budget (Derived / Inferred)

Both limits are wider than the tolerances stated on the child sheets. With the angular tolerances added linearly (a worst-case assumption; the sheet gives no combination rule):

Wheel Assembly limit (10.239) Child angular tolerances Sum Remainder
Drive wheel (Schaltrad, 10.218) ±4°10′ ±1° (star point to axle groove, 10.216-2) + ±1° (nose to outer contour, 10.218-2) ±2°00′ 2°10′
Locking wheel (Sperrad, 10.222) ±3°50′ ±1° (star point to axle groove) + ±40′ (nose rotation, 10.222-2) ±1°40′ 2°10′

The two remainders are identical, 2°10′ (Derived). That suggests the assembly limits were built from the child tolerances plus one common allowance for the wheel-to-axle fit and clocking, which the sheet does not state (Inferred). The wheel's 2° nose offset on 10.222 is a nominal rotation, not part of the tolerance. As a share of the 72° star pitch, the full bands (8°20′ and 7°40′) are 11.6 % and 10.6 % (Derived). The mapping of Schaltrad and Sperrad to 10.218 and 10.222 is itself Inferred (see above), as is the linear addition.

Axial layout of the elevation (Derived, scan-measured)

The elevation carries no dimension, so positions were measured on a 400-dpi render of p247. The scale was set from features whose size is known: the 1.5 mm gear pitch and the 0.5 mm disc thickness of the 10.218 stack, the 1.1 mm relief and the 8.65 mm dimension of 10.216-2, and the Ø3.8 star. These give 33.4–34.7 px/mm on the render, so the mean 34.3 px/mm is good to about ±2 %, or ±0.15–0.4 mm at the positions below; add the printed line width. Cross-checks (Derived).

  • The neck is drawn from 7.5 to 8.6 mm, matching the 1.1 relief ending at 8.65 mm on 10.216-2.
  • 10.218: the top of the top gear is 1.96 mm below the sleeve top (10.020 upper section 2.0 mm); the three gears and two spacers plus the 1.8 mm 10.041 end at 19.7 mm, 7.24 mm below the sleeve top against 2.0 + 5.3 = 7.3 mm from the child sheets. The foot below that, about 0.35 mm on the scan, is the sleeve's end beyond the 10.041: the child audit's 0.5 mm "unallocated" remainder (Part-Dimensions-10218.md) appears to be that projecting end, which the sheet's swaging note would use (Inferred).
  • 10.222: the ratchet disc measures 0.5 mm thick, and the ring below it is 1.6 mm (5.5–7.1), matching the 10.141 press sleeve. The Ø2.7 body above the disc is drawn 2.0 mm long, against the 2.55 mm flange on 10.056-2, and the whole sleeve stack is drawn about 4.5 mm long (3.0–7.5) against 5.05 mm; about 0.5 mm short, more than the scan error. Either the drawing is schematic or the flange is drawn short (Unverified).
  • The shaft is drawn 23.7 mm below the star against 49.6 ±0.1 mm on 10.216-2, so it is cut short with a break line and its length is not to scale.

The spacing between the ones-lock and the setting wheel is not dimensioned; on the scan the bottom face of the 10.222 ratchet disc is 8.9 mm above the top face of the first 10.218 gear (Derived, scan-measured).

An angular limit of ±4°10′ at a 3× wheel spans a larger arc at the tooth tips than at 1× (arc = radius × angle), so the same angle is a looser fit in mm terms; per the project's tolerance decision the printed-prototype tolerance comes from measurement, not from these numbers.
