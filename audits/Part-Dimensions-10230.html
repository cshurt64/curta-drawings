What the two 105° and the 0,8 measure (Inferred)

Both 105° arcs run from the horizontal centreline to a slanted line through the sleeve wall, and the end view shows three dashed rectangles on the sleeve ring: one on the centreline and one on each of the two slanted lines. Those are the three cuts the elevation calls 3 Einschnitte gebördelt, and the bottom of the elevation shows three tabs at the sleeve end. So the cuts sit at 0°, +105° and −105° — spaced 105°, 105° and 150° — and the 0,8 is the width of one cut. This is the same pair of marks the 10.217 audit left as datum meaning Unverified; the drawing sheets agree and this reading is consistent with both, but no sheet states it, so it stays Inferred. The V-shaped lug (the Kerbnase) sits opposite, on the left.

Open points

  1. The three-cut geometry (Inferred). See above; a fresh reading of the 10.140 sheet's notch and the wheel's lug against the cuts would settle it.
  2. Finished wheel-to-shaft fit (Unverified). The 10.038 bore is J8 (Ø2.293–2.307), a transition fit on the 10.140 h7 section (up to 7 µm interference, up to 17 µm clearance). This sheet has the bore finished by broaching to a gauge after assembly, so the finished fit is set by the gauge. (An earlier version of this note read a 21–45 µm interference from a misread bore; corrected 2026-10-10.)
  3. Gruppe field (Confirmed, and a correction). It reads Grundkörper while the parent 10.238 is titled Kompl. Treibelement II. So Gruppe does not name the parent's title here, which weakens the reading recorded on the 10.233 sheet. Gruppe looks like the drawing group a sheet belongs to; it coincides with a parent's title only where the parent heads that group.
  4. Machine-Facts open item 8 (quantity 3, not 15). The title block reading here (Stück p.Masch. 3) is a third independent source for 3.
