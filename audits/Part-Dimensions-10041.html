The 5.400 mm axial length confirms that the 6.600 mm source part preserved in the current 10.217 V5 exports is too long by 1.200 mm. The project later adopted 6.600 mm deliberately for the printed replica in V8 and the 10.218/10.219 V9 candidates, as a substitute for the factory crimp-tab retention (a project decision); 5.400 mm remains the factory 3× fact.

The current sheet does not specify a blanket general angular tolerance. Its revision note explicitly says the earlier 0.1 mm and 90° callouts were removed.

Assembly relevance

The title block associates 10.041 with complete setting-wheel assemblies 10.217, 10.218 and 10.219. At 3×, its 5.400 mm axial length contributes to the assembly stack and its Ø6.900 H8 bore interfaces with the mating sleeve/hub. These are inherited component dimensions, not direct assembly callouts.
