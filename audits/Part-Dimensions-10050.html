Derived checks (estimates, not sheet facts). (1) Inside 8.2 + wire 0.6 = 8.8 mm mean diameter = 2 × r4.4: the printed radius and diameter agree, which is how the Ø8.2 is read as an inside diameter. Outside diameter 9.4 mm (not printed). (2) Travel: 4 active coils × 2.31 mm = 9.24 mm, the larger travel on the graph. (3) Length: 3.3 solid + 9.24 travel = 12.54 mm, matching the printed free length of about 13. (4) Rate: 0.32 kg / 6.6 mm = 0.0485 kg/mm and 0.45 kg / 9.24 mm = 0.0487 kg/mm, so the two load points lie on one line (about 0.48 N/mm). (5) Textbook rate k = G·d⁴ / (8·D³·n) with d = 0.6, D = 8.8, n = 4 and an assumed G of 78 GPa gives about 0.46 N/mm, within 4% of the drawn 0.48 N/mm. G is assumed. All five are estimates; the checks agree so well that the sheet reads as internally consistent.
