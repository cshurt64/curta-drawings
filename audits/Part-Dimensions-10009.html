Open points

  1. 3-H7 +9/0 (Unverified). ISO 286 gives +10/0 at 3 mm. The sheet's table is 1 µm tighter; the same pattern appears on 10.072. Closes the item left open on 10.206 (206-5).
  2. Two thicknesses at one flange (Unverified). 2.05 ±30 and 2.1 ±10 are both printed at the flange; the sheet does not say what the second spans (the flange plus the lip in detail C is a candidate).
  3. What several section-view dimensions span (Unverified). 9.7 ±200, 7, 8.1, 14.5 ±20, 4.8, 6.7 0/−30, 2.5 and 0.7. The dense section has no labels for the faces.
  4. The star on *3ø-H7, *5.4ø-f6, *16-h6 (Unverified). Read as marking dimensions checked in inspection.
  5. A transitional design (Confirmed note, Unverified meaning). The note Schlitz 2-H9 u. Ansenkung 80° says the slot and countersink are a transitional design until tooth segments with the large pitch and the old locking discs are used up; which variant (2 H9 slot or the 2 +50/+10 notch) is the current one is not stated.
  6. Scan scale. The printed 5:1 does not match the scan (about 3.5:1); take every value from the callouts.
