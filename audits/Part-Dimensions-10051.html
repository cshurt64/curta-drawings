Revision (a), change 276

The revision line reads Maß 1,9-D9 in 1,9 +50/+20; Silberstahl Ø3 in Messing 58 geändert (dimension 1,9-D9 changed to 1,9 +50/+20; silver steel Ø3 changed to brass 58), marked 2 X, change no. 276, 14.12.49 (drawn 23.6.49). So an ISO D9 was replaced by explicit limits, and the material changed from silver steel to brass. ISO D9 at 1.9 mm would be EI = +20, IT9 = 25 → +20/+45 µm (Derived); the explicit +20/+50 is 5 µm wider at the top. The length is the only toleranced axial dimension on the sleeve, so the stack on the guide sleeve depends on it (see Part-Dimensions-10055.md).

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size.

Ø6 mm (3× of Ø2) sits exactly on the upper boundary of the "over 3 through 6 mm" step, so it belongs to that step; Ø6.001 would fall in the next. None of this is a fabrication tolerance; per the project's tolerance decision printed prototype fits come from measurement.
