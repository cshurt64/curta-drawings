10.065-5 Quotient-Setter Post — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.065, Quotienteneinstellsäule (quotient-setter post; the service manual's factory English calls it the reversing lever shaft, manual p49 — see Machine-Facts.md §2a for why this is the quotient/revolution-counter direction control, not the subtraction control), drawing 10.065-5, current; Ersatz für 10.065-4. Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 85 (viewer page 85). Scale 3:1 (reference view M 1:1); stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm. Drawn 15.6.1950; two changes: index 3 in 4 (no. 197, 15.6.50) and index 4 in 5 (no. 228, 22.8.50). Read at 300 dpi on 2026-09-26.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.241 (complete quotient setter, factory English "reversing lever complete with shaft"); Stück p.Masch. 1, agreeing with the callout on p249 and BOM sheet 4 (p204).
  • Material: nichtrost. Stahl RRMD Ø2,5-h8 (stainless steel, RRMD grade, drawn bar already at Ø2.5 h8).
  • General tolerance: ±100 µm linear, ±30′ angular. Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces; 50 µm radial on unmarked, side runout a dash. Finish: ▽▽, with ▽▽▽ in brackets.
  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved readings Unverified. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.
