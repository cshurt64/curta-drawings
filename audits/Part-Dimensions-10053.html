Relation to the ratchet wheel 10.049

Both are five-point stars of the same 72° pitch with a Ø2.3 bore and a 90° notch, and both fit the same guide sleeve 10.055 (see Part-Dimensions-10055.md). The drive wheel is 0.6 ±0.020 mm thick (the ratchet wheel 0.5 ±0.020), 4.4 +0.050/0 across (4.4 ±0.020), with a 3.3 root circle and a smoother tooth form (0.75 r roots); the ratchet wheel has a slit at each tip. The rotation of the notch to the outer contour is ±30′ per tooth here, ±20′ on 10.049, and the 10.220 assembly gives ±1° for this wheel against 2° ±40′ for 10.049. On the sheet, "tooth curve, hole and nose re-passivated" applies.

Reading this sheet closes the stack question left by Part-Dimensions-10055.md: 1.9 (10.051) + 1.3 (10.054) + 0.5 (10.049) + 0.6 (10.053) = 4.3 mm against the 4.8 mm reduced length, so 0.5 mm remains unaccounted for (Derived; not a tolerance stack-up, which the sheets' general tolerances would only widen).

Machine-Facts §4 recorded the material as phosphor bronze 0.6; the sheet and the BOM master say stainless hardenable strip steel 0.6 ±20 µm, so §4 is corrected.
