ISO 286 fit cases

ISO 286-1 defines the system; ISO 286-2 tabulates the deviations. Both cases lie in the "over 1 through 3 mm" step.

Feature Case IT / fundamental deviation ISO limits Sheet fit table Status
Head 2 h8 IT8 = 14 µm; es = 0 1.986–2.000 mm 0/−14 Confirmed; agrees
Shank 1 f8 IT8 = 14 µm; es = −6 µm (f), ei = −20 µm 0.980–0.994 mm −7/−21 Confirmed callout; the sheet is 1 µm lower than ISO on both limits (Unverified)

For h, es = 0 and the zone is below nominal. For f the whole zone is below nominal with a clearance gap. The callout prints a capital F8 on the shank while the fit table prints lowercase f8 with negative deviations; a shaft with negative deviations is an f, so it is read as 1 f8 (Unverified).
