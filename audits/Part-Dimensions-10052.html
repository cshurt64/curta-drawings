Blank versus finished part (page 257)

This sheet is the turned blank: a Ø4 ±50 µm × 7.2 mm cylinder of brass Ms 58 with a Ø2 0/−20 µm bore taper of 4 %. The finished five-point star is drawn on page 257, numbered 105.254 and titled Fünfzack, in Ms 58 Pb, 17 per machine, parents 10.207/08/09/216, scale 10:1 and 30:1, drawn 12.12.1950 as read (later than this sheet's 15.5.1950). The index described that sheet as an older 105.xxx series; its date does not support "older" (Unverified reading of the year).

Values read from page 257, Confirmed as read, recorded here because the sheet had no entry: overall height 6.95 (6.19, 6.62 and 6.95 are Einstellmass set-up dimensions for a measuring fixture with the dial gauge zeroed), a 4.35 ±20 µm section, Ø3.8 ±20 and Ø3 ±20 (both marked *), bore Ø2 0/−20, taper 4 %, five teeth at 72°, theoretical Ø2.05 and Ø1.1 +20/0 measuring circles, tooth angle 20°, radii 0.5 and 0.6, 45° measuring axis, general ±50 µm. It has no side panel, guide or audit of its own yet.

So the blank's 7.2 mm becomes 6.95 mm finished (0.25 mm removed) and its Ø4 becomes Ø3.8 across the teeth.
