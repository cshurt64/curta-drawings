The two fit-table entries (ISO 286-1 system, ISO 286-2 deviations) — recalculated

  • 10 H7. 10 mm is the upper boundary of the 6–10 mm step, so it belongs to that step: IT7 = 15 µm, EI = 0, zone above nominal ⇒ Ø10.000–10.015. This matches the sheet's +15/0 exactly (Derived, matches).
  • 1 S7. 1 mm is in the up to 3 mm step: IT7 = 10 µm and the ISO fundamental deviation for S gives ES = −14, EI = −24 ⇒ Ø0.976–0.986. The sheet prints −13 / −22 (Ø0.978–0.987) — 1–2 µm shifted, the same sub-3 mm older-table pattern recorded in Machine-Facts.md §3 (Derived, differs by 1–2 µm).

Neither entry has a callout on the printed view except the Ø1 S7 note.

The ball count is inferred, and the allocation does not close

The sheet labels one ball. Counting radial arms on the plan view gives 17, matching the 17 digit windows. BOM sheet 9 (p211) lists Kugel Ø 2 at 26 per machine with no remark. The recorded parents are 10.201 ×17, 10.240 ×1 and 10.241 ×1 = 19, leaving 7 of the 26 with no recorded parent (Derived). Either other assemblies use Ø2 balls that have not been read yet, or the 17 here is wrong. That is Unverified, and is the one quantity in this assembly that is not settled.

Calculated and inferred stack-ups

None can be made. The section shows the stack but prints no axial dimension, and the scan is reduced from the original sheet with no printed length to calibrate against. The number-roll axial stack and the window pitch are on the children and on 10.243-1. A stack-up for this assembly would rest on scaled scan pixels, so none is recorded.

Open points

  1. The Ø2 ball quantity (Inferred, 7 unallocated). See above.
  2. 10-H7 with no callout (Unverified). The feature it dimensions is not printed on the view; the counter-sleeve bore or a bore in 10.243 are candidates, none checked.
  3. Which part carries the Ø1 S7 hole (Unverified). 10.144 has Ø1 holes but carries H7 slots, not S7.
  4. The position numbers 2–5 (Unverified).
  5. 10.004 material (Confirmed, and a correction). Machine-Facts.md §4 recorded Phosphor bronze 0.14; both the part's own sheet (10.004-2, p5) and BOM sheet 1 (p201) read hardenable stainless band steel, 0.1 ±10 µ, Brinell 230–240. Corrected there.
  6. The mm after the dash (Confirmed, odd). The general linear tolerance prints — mm; the sheet still carries an Abmasse in μ stamp.
