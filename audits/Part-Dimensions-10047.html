Derived checks (estimates, not sheet facts). (1) Mean diameter 0.9 − 0.15 = 0.75 mm, as printed. (2) Four active coils at 0.145 mm each give 0.58 mm of travel. (3) A textbook rate k = G·d⁴ / (8·D³·n) with d = 0.15 mm, D = 0.75 mm, n = 4 and an assumed G of 78 GPa gives about 2.9 N/mm, so 0.58 mm of travel would be about 1.7 N (about 170 gf). G is assumed, and the sheet states no load, so this is only an order of magnitude.
