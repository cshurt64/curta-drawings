Fit with the setting axle 10.061 (Derived)

The central bore is Ø2.5 D9 (+20/+45 µm: 2.520–2.545) and the setting axle 10.061 is Ø2.5 h6 (0/−7 µm on its sheet; ISO −6): 2.493–2.500. The knob slides over the axle: minimum clearance 20 µm, maximum 52 µm (2.545 − 2.493), a running fit (Derived; pairing Inferred, since the knob rides the setting axle in the housing slot). The Ø2 E8 cross hole and the C9 slot are separate features; their mates are not printed on this sheet. The note says the D9 and E8 bores are checked after anodizing, so the sizes are final coated sizes.

Notches and the 6° pitch

The rim carries 60 notches at 6° pitch (Derived from the note entspricht 60 Kerben and the 6° callout; 360/6 = 60). This ties to the 8-position setting axle only through the setting display, not dimensionally.

Revision state

The drawing number is printed 10.057-5 with the 5 struck and a 6 written over it, but the revision table lists only index 4→5 (change 223). The 5→6 change is not recorded on the sheet (Unverified); the catalog and index treat this sheet as revision 6.

Type and quantity

Type IIa 12, Type I 8: 12 knobs on the Type IIa machine and 8 on Type I, which is the type stamped on every sheet of this set (the Curta Type I has 8 setting positions). No 12-position machine is in scope.
