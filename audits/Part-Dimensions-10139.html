Selective-fit grading

No ISO 286 letter fit is used. Instead the washer's thickness, labelled a, is graded into eight classes in 0.02 mm steps:

Pos. Thickness a (mm)
1 0.06
2 0.08
3 0.10
4 0.12
5 0.14
6 0.16
7 0.18
8 0.20

Overall range 0.06–0.20 mm across the eight grades — the same selective-fit convention used on 10.064, 10.077, 10.099, 10.120 and 10.135 elsewhere in this drawing set. The number of grades (8) coincides with the confirmed per-machine quantity (also 8), but the two are read as independent facts here: eight washers are installed (one per setting knob) and each is separately selected from among eight thickness grades depending on the individual knob's fit-up.
