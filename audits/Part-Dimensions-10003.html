Straightness at 3×

The sheet's straightness limit is 25 µm over 85 mm, about 0.3 ‰ (Derived). Machine-Facts §3 already records the 3× target as ≤0.075 mm over 255 mm, which is the same ratio, 0.29 ‰, scaled with the length. The main axle is the one part whose accuracy is a length ratio, not a size, so it scales with the length even under the project's process-based tolerance rule.

Fits with the drum 10.001 and the bearing bores

The Ø3 h7 axle meets the drum's J8 bore as a transition fit and its U8 bore as a press fit; see Part-Dimensions-10001.md. Other mating bores (tens bell, 10.009, Ø3 H7 in Machine-Facts §4a) are on their own sheets.

Where-used

The sheet's own Kompl. Nr. names 10.223 (Confirmed). There is exactly one main axle per machine (Stück 1, BOM sheet 1), so it has one parent, 10.223; it is not also a child of the drum assembly 10.213, whose sheet has no 10.003 leader. It passes through the drum, which is a mating relationship, not containment (Confirmed).

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size; a scaled nominal exactly on a size-step boundary belongs to the lower step.
