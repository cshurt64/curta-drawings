The Ø2.5 H9 bore (ISO 286-1 system, ISO 286-2 deviations)

2.5 mm lies in the up to 3 mm step: IT9 = 25 µm, EI = 0 (a hole H), zone above nominal ⇒ Ø2.500–2.525, matching the sheet's +25/0 (Derived, agrees). The bushing's own sheet dimensions only a Ø2.1 +50/0 relief; the Ø2.5 bore is therefore finished in the assembly (the drawing shows it as the through-bore) — Inferred.
