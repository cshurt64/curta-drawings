The outer profile. Section A–A shows a sawtooth on the outside: eight teeth at a 1.2 mm pitch (8 × 1,2 = 9,6), with 0.7 mm flats at each end and 90° flanks; section B–B shows the same profile with 100° flanks. Whether this is a thread, a ratchet form or a rounded relief is not named on the sheet (Unverified). The 120-tooth knurl on the outside of the collar is a separate feature.
