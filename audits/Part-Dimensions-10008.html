Cross holes and screws (Derived, from two sheets)

Two M1 cross holes, 2.5 ±0.030 mm apart, sit 13 ±0.050 mm from the top end and 1.5 mm from the bottom end (17 − 13 − 2.5, Derived). The parent sheet 10.226-3 clamps each axle with two M1 × 3 cylinder-head screws and the BOM lists 34 of them: 2 per axle × 17 = 34, so each cross hole takes one screw (Derived; the pairing of hole and screw is not printed). The axle's fit in the body's 2-M7 slot is derived in Part-Dimensions-10226.md: a transition fit, clearance 7 to interference 9 µm by the sheet values.
