10.100-3 Tens Tooth Disc, Revolution Counter — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.100, Zehnerzahnscheibe für Umdrehungs-Werk (tens tooth disc for the revolution counter, UW), drawing 10.100-3, current; Ersatz für 10.100-2 (105.091), so it descends from the old-series drawing 105.091 through 10.100-2. Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 127 (viewer page 127). Scale 5:1 (reference view M1:1); stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm. Drawn 26.1.50; the change block is empty. Read at 300 dpi on 2026-09-27.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.206 (complete tens bell); Stück p.Masch. 1, agreeing with the callout on p197 and BOM sheet 5 (p207).
  • Material: Neusilber 0,5 ±10 Brin. 230–240 (nickel silver, 0.5 mm ±0.010, Brinell hardness 230–240) — the same material family already recorded in Machine-Facts.md §4/§5 for the tens tooth and locking discs (10.100–10.107).
  • General tolerance: ±100 µm linear, ±30′ angular. Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces; dashes for unmarked. No finish, surface or heat treatment stated.
  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved readings Unverified. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.
