Policy note (2026-10-10). The ISO columns above are reference only: under a project decision (addendum 2026-10-10) the machined sleeve holds the factory absolute bands (0/−60, 0/−10, +14/0 µm on the diameters and ±20 µm on the two called-out lengths), not the 3× ISO widths. This sheet states no general tolerance; the project nevertheless carries ±100 µm for its untoleranced dimensions because the similar sleeves (10.017, 10.020, 10.032, 10.055) state it. That is a project convention, not a value printed on this drawing.

Ø6 mm (3× of Ø2) sits exactly on the upper boundary of the "over 3 through 6 mm" step, so it belongs to that step; Ø6.001 would fall in the next. None of this is a fabrication tolerance; per the project's tolerance decision printed prototype fits come from measurement.
