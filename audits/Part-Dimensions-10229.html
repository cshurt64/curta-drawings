Fit table 8-N8 −3/−25 — Confirmed

ISO 286 case: Ø8 N8. ISO 286-1 defines the system and ISO 286-2 tabulates deviations. Ø8 is in the >6–10 mm step. IT8 = 22 µm. For hole N up to IT8, ES = −ei(n) + Δ, with the n-shaft ei = +10 µm at this step and Δ = IT8 − IT7 = 22 − 15 = 7 µm, so ES = −3 µm and EI = −3 − 22 = −25 µm. That gives Ø7.975–7.997 mm, agreeing with the sheet's −3/−25 (Derived).

The bushing's Ø9 p7 (+30/+15) goes into the sleeve's Ø9 H7 (+15/0): both IT7 = 15 µm at the >6–10 mm step, p has ei = +15 µm, so the fit runs from 0 to 30 µm interference (max 30 − 0, min 15 − 15). The bushing's bore is Ø7.8, smaller than the assembly's Ø8 N8, so the Ø8 N8 is finished after pressing (Inferred; the star marks it as a runout-controlled surface). IT7 for the Ø11 H7 and Ø12 j7 is 18 µm at the >10–18 mm step, agreeing with the sheet's +18/0 and +12/−6.

Calculated and inferred stack-ups

The bushing is 4.3 long and sits in a step whose depth is 4.6 −0/−50, so about 0.3 mm (0.25–0.35) of the step is left beyond it (Inferred; the sheet does not say the bushing seats in that step).
