10.243-1 Counter Assembly, Stage I — assembly dimensional audit and 3× reference

Scope and source status

  • Assembly: 10.243, Zählwerk Stufe I (counter, stage I), drawing revision 10.243-1, current. Identity verified against Drawing-Index.md: page 251 is 10.243‑1 Zählwerk Stufe I, and the sheet's own title block agrees. It is a plan-and-section assembly at scale 5:1, with a 10:1 profile detail.
  • Primary source: original Contina engineering drawing, drawing-set PDF page 251 / drawing-viewer page 251, rasterized at 400–800 dpi. Unlike the child sheets, this sheet has no Abmasse in μ stamp. Its tolerance block is labelled in mm and its ±0,2 mm note is written in millimetres, so its dimensions are read as millimetres; the child sheets (10.088-2, 10.089-5, 10.008-4) do carry the µm convention.
  • Child sources read for this audit: 10.088-2 Counter ring (page 115), 10.089-5 Counter cap (page 116), 10.008-4 Counter-wheel axle (page 14), the number rolls 10.043-6 / 10.045-6, and the pin 10.021-4 (audited in Part-Dimensions-10043.md, Part-Dimensions-10045.md and Part-Dimensions-10203.md). The counter body 10.040-5 was viewed but is not audited, and the assembly 10.226-3 was not opened for this audit.
  • Labels: each value is one of direct assembly callout (printed on 10.243-1), inherited component dimension (printed on a child sheet), or calculated / inferred stack-up. Facts are Confirmed, recalculated values Derived, unresolved readings Unverified, judgement calls Inferred.
  • ISO limits: Derived from ISO 286-1 (system) and ISO 286-2 (tables).

Official standards references: ISO 286-1 and ISO 286-2.

Two of the four sheet entries match ISO 286-2 exactly (the H8 pair) and two are 1 µm off (h6 and F8), as is the roll's P7 entry. Reading further sheets (see Part-Dimensions-10226.md) shows this is a consistent pattern, not misreading: at sizes up to 3 mm the drawings' zone widths are 7 µm for grade 6 and 9 µm for grade 7 (ISO: 6 and 10), and grade 8 matches, which is why the H8 pair agrees. Cause Unverified (probably an older ISA-based table). An earlier version of this paragraph said the differences were not table-wide; that was wrong.

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. The "simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted for any process, and no fit is inherited from the factory table. Where a callout carries an ISO fit code, the code is re-applied at the scaled basic size and the limits recalculated. A 3× table is not a factory specification, and the general and non-ISO tolerances are not silently scaled.

Thread pair M150 × 1.5 by plain scaling — — Not adopted: threads do not scale by rule; pitch and form are a project decision

The 3× axle and hub bore, and the 3× slot, sit exactly on the upper boundary of the "over 3 through 6 mm" ISO size step (6.000 mm belongs to it; 6.001 mm would fall in the next step). The 3× cap bore falls in a larger step, so its IT8 grows from 39 to 63 µm.

None of this is a fabrication tolerance. A printed FDM part cannot hold these limits; per the project's tolerance decision printed prototype fits come from measurement.
