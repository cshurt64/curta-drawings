Ratchet wheel and star: how they relate

The star is 4.4 ±20 µm across with five concave sides (5 r), a 90° ±10′ notch and a 0.15 ±20 µm slit at one tip. Its 72° pitch is 360°/5. A Verdrehung von Nase zur Aussenkontur note limits the notch ("nose") rotation to each arc centre to 2° ±20′. It engages the five-point star 10.052 on its shaft (Machine-Facts §2 and the transmission-shaft sheets) — the star is the 6.95 mm finished part on page 257, not the blank.

Material is stainless hardenable strip steel from the firm Mathey (Brinell 230–240), re-passivated, matching the BOM. Machine-Facts §4 had it as beryllium bronze; corrected.
