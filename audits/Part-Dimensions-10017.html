Project 3× dimensions with ISO grades recalculated

These values triple the nominal geometry, then recalculate the same ISO letter and grade at the new basic-size step. They do not triple the original IT width. Per the requested working convention, the two non-ISO axial dimensions retain the drawing's ±100 µm general tolerance; that convention remains a project choice rather than a factory specification.

Assembly relevance

Drawing 10.217-2 uses 10.017 in the complete setting wheel. For an assembly dimension audit, the 14.100 mm overall and 8.100 mm lower length above are inherited component dimensions, not direct 10.217 assembly callouts. They must be combined with the separately sourced 10.038 drive-wheel and 10.041 spacer dimensions to validate the axial stack; no stack result should be presented as factory-specified unless the assembly drawing directly controls it.
