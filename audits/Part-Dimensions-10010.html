Open points

  1. 1.8-F8 +21/+7 (Unverified). ISO 286-2 gives +20/+6 for F8 at 1.8 mm; the sheet is 1 µm above on both limits.
  2. What 3.8 and 4.8 measure to (resolved 2026-10-04, Derived from a 220 dpi raster read). 3.8 runs from the left edge and 4.8 from the notch's centre, both to the vertical centre line through the slot. The r6.5 curve is concentric with the r2.1 notch, centred 1.0 mm left of the left edge at mid-height; its rightmost point meets the 5.5 mm overall width and its top and bottom corners fall about 2.1 mm inside it. Pixel measurements agree within about 0.05 mm.
  3. The part's name. The catalog and BOM say Umsteuerungsnase (reversing nose); the service manual's function for it is the step drum positioning tongue (Machine-Facts §2a). Both names are kept.
