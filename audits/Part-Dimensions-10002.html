Correction. Machine-Facts.md recorded this outside diameter as Ø45.4 h7 (in the drawing table and the fit table). The sheet reads 45,4 φ-n7 with fit-table entry +42/+17; I confirmed both at high zoom. h7 would be 0/−25; n7 is a positive, interference-side fit. The counterpart bore on the lower housing 10.062 is the mating interface and was not checked here (Unverified).
