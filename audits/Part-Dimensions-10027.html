ISO 286 fit cases

ISO 286-1 defines the system; ISO 286-2 tabulates the deviations. Both cases lie in the "over 1 through 3 mm" step.

Feature Case IT / fundamental deviation ISO limits Sheet fit table Status
Top spigot Ø1.8 j7 IT7 = 10 µm; j7 for this step: +6/−4 1.796–1.806 mm +7/−2 Confirmed callout; the sheet differs from ISO (Unverified)
Press-fit end Ø2 u7 IT7 = 10 µm; u: ei = +18 µm, es = +28 µm 2.018–2.028 mm +27/+18 Confirmed callout; lower limit agrees, upper is 1 µm lower

j straddles the nominal size; u puts the whole zone above nominal, giving an interference fit against an H hole. This closes the open point in Part-Dimensions-10227.md about the u7 entry (the other, 1.8-j7, is the entry it called partly cropped).

A pattern across sheets. Every IT7 reading at 1–3 mm on these sheets is 9 µm wide, where ISO 286-2 gives 10: 10.009 (3 H7, +9/0), 10.013 (3 N7, −4/−13), 10.020 (2.3 h7, 0/−9), 10.072 (3 h7, 0/−9) and here (2 u7, +27/+18; 1.8 j7, +7/−2). The factory sheets predate ISO 286 (1962 onward) and were probably drawn to the earlier ISA tolerance tables, which had IT7 at 1–3 mm as about 9 µm. That is Inferred from the numbers and not stated on any sheet.
