10.085-5 CURTA Nameplate Sleeve — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.085, Manschette (cuff/sleeve; the catalog lists it as "Sleeve/collar" — this is the sleeve carrying the engraved CURTA lettering), drawing 10.085-5, current; Ersatz für 10.085-4. Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 111 (viewer page 111). Three scales on one sheet: 1:1 (a small sectional detail), 5:1 (the developed/unrolled lettering view, Abwicklung), and 10:1 (section A–A). Stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm. Drawn 1.12.1950; one change: index 4 in 5 (no. 311). Read at 300 dpi on 2026-09-26.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.234 (base assembly stage III); Stück p.Masch. 1, agreeing with the callout on p241 and BOM sheet 5 (p207).
  • Material: Anticorodalrohr B 50/3 (Anticorodal tube B, a hardenable aluminium alloy, 50 mm outside by 3 mm wall). This corrects Machine-Facts.md §4, which read Anticorodal tube 48/2. Surface treatment: rein sandgestrahlt, schwarz eloxiert / graviert fähig (purely sand-blasted, black anodized, suitable for engraving) — the finish the CURTA lettering is cut into.
  • General tolerance: ±100 µm linear, ±30′ angular. Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces; unmarked side runout 50 µm, unmarked radial runout not stated. Finish: ▽▽, with ▽▽▽ in brackets.

The two witness marks. Beide Pfeile gleich gross und in gleicher Höhe (both arrows the same size and at the same height): two raised arrow marks on the rim, "1. Pfeil" and "2. Pfeil," used to index the lettering angularly around the sleeve during engraving. Their angular positions (130°, 200°) and the lettering's own span (70°, glossed as the sum of the two right-end developed lengths, 35°) let the two be cross-checked: 130° + 70° = 200°, so the lettering span plus the gap between marks accounts for the full positioning (Derived, consistent with the printed numbers).
