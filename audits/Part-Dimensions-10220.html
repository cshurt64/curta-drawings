Stack, fits and angles (Derived / Inferred)

The five components sit on one sleeve axis. The three sleeves (10.055 flange sleeve, 10.051 spacer, 10.054 crimped press-fit sleeve) carry the two wheels; the wheels' Ø2.3 bores (JS8) ride on the 10.055 Ø2.3 h7 shaft as a transition fit (clearance to +16 µm, interference to 7 µm; Part-Dimensions-10055.md). Axial thicknesses from the part sheets: 10.051 1.9 (+0.050/+0.020), 10.054 1.3, 10.049 0.5 ±0.020, 10.053 0.6 ±0.020 — 4.3 mm against the 4.8 mm reduced sleeve length, so 0.5 mm is not accounted for on the sheets (Unverified). The sheet does not dimension the axial layout.

The two wheels are rotated relative to each other (the end view shows one wheel's teeth between the other's): 105° marks in the end view relate their angular positions, Unverified. The nose-rotation limits differ: ratchet wheel 2° ±40′, drive wheel ±1°, against the parts' own ±20′ and ±30′ limits, so the assembly controls are looser than the components'. The bore and notch are broached after assembly to a calibre gauge, which is why no bore fit is stated here.
