Blank versus finished part

This sheet is the finished part of the BOM item 10.052 (Fünfzack, 17 per machine); Part-Dimensions-10052.md audits the turned blank (10.052-4, Fünfzackdrehling, Ø4 ±50 µm × 7.2 mm, bore Ø2 0/−20 µm). Comparing the two:

Quantity Blank (10.052-4) Finished (105.254) Difference
Length / height 7.2 mm 6.95 mm 0.25 mm removed (Derived)
Diameter Ø4 ±50 µm Ø3.8 ±20 µm body; Ø3 ±20 µm toothed head 0.2 mm and 1.0 mm removed (Derived)
Bore Ø2 0/−20 µm, 4 % taper Ø2 0/−20 µm, 4 % taper identical
Material Ms 58 Ms 58 Pb lead added on the finished sheet (Unverified which is correct; BOM says 58 Pb)

The Ø3.8 body is 4.35 ±0.020 mm long, so the Ø3 toothed head is 6.95 − 4.35 = 2.60 mm tall (Derived). The five teeth are milled into that head at 72° pitch. The checking method is on the sheet: Einstellmasse gelten für Messvorrichtung (Messuhr auf 0 gestellt) — the set-up dimensions 6.19, 6.62 and 6.95 (and 1.49, 1.19) apply to a measuring fixture with the dial gauge set to zero. That is an inspection procedure, not a part dimension. Sheet-number oddity: the drawing carries the series number 105.254, where the other sheets use 10.xxx; its date (12.12.1950) is later than the blank's (15.5.1950).

Where-used: the assemblies list this part as 10.052, so the edges stay on 10.052; adding 105.254 edges would double-count the 17.
