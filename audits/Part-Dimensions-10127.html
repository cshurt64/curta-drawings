10.127-2 Protective-Sleeve Upper Part — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.127, Schutzhülsenoberteil (protective-sleeve upper part), drawing 10.127-2, current; replaces 10.127-1 (page 158, superseded). Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 159 (viewer page 159). Scale 1:1; stamped Abmasse in µ = 1/1000 mm. Drawn 24 May 1950; change 183 changed an unnamed number in index 1, and change 315 changed index 1 to index 2 on 1 Dec 1950. Read at 400 dpi on 2026-09-28.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.235; Stück p.Masch. 1. This agrees with the callout on p243 and BOM/Machine- Facts data and is already recorded in Assembly-Links.csv as 10.235,10.127,1 (Confirmed).
  • Material: Peraluman 1,5 (1.5 mm Peraluman sheet), agreeing with Machine-Facts.md §4/§728.
  • General tolerance: linear and angular fields are dashes; no default tolerance is stated. Marked radial and axial runout are 20 µm; unmarked runout fields are dashes. Predominant finish says siehe oben (see above), referring to Aussenpartien und Gewinde hochglanz poliert! (outer surfaces and thread polished to a high gloss). No separate surface or heat treatment is stated on this part sheet; the black anodizing belongs to assembly 10.235.
  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved feature associations Unverified. The 3× table is a geometric reference, not a factory specification.

ISO 286 fit cases

There is no ISO 286 letter fit on this sheet. The explicit unilateral tolerances are ordinary drawing tolerances, and Ø56.5 n. Lehre means controlled by gauge, not ISO shaft zone n.

Open points

  1. Top-detail associations (Unverified). The 8, Ø16, and 4 values are legible, but the scan does not label the referenced faces independently enough to state their functions more narrowly without guessing.
  2. Thread gauge and terminology (Unverified). The sheet gives Ø56.5 n. Lehre but no gauge limits. It calls the 19.05 value Steigung on a three-start thread; assembly records treat this as lead, but this sheet alone does not explicitly distinguish lead from per-start pitch.
  3. Approximate Ø41 association (Unverified). The value is legible in plan view; the target contour is not named.
  4. Change 183 (Unverified). “Number changed in index 1” does not identify the affected dimension.
