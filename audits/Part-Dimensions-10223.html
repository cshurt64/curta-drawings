The Ø0.98 H6 hole (ISO 286-1 system, ISO 286-2 deviations)

0.98 mm lies in the up to 3 mm step: IT6 = 6 µm, EI = 0 (a hole H), zone above nominal ⇒ Ø0.980–0.986. The fit table on the sheet prints +7/0 (Ø0.980–0.987): 1 µm looser than ISO — the same sub-3 mm older-table pattern recorded in Machine-Facts.md §3 (Derived, differs by 1 µm).

The spring pin quantity is a source conflict

The sheet draws one pin (Section B-B). BOM sheet 9 lists Schwerspannstift S 1×5,5 at 3 per machine, remark Hauptachse — and 10.223 is one per machine. Both are printed. Three holes appear on the assembly (A-A, B-B, C-C), which would fit three pins, but the A-A hole is the crank's conical hole and the C-C hole is Ø0.98 H6, not obviously a pin hole. Not resolved here; Assembly-Links.csv keeps 1 with status Inferred and flags the conflict, per Where-Used-Method.md's "stop and ask" rule.

Calculated and inferred stack-ups

The lengths on the elevation come from a drawing cut with break lines, so they do not add up to the 85 mm of 10.003 and nothing can be measured off the scan. A stack of 65.6 / 65.1 / 16.3 / 2 mm cannot be closed because the start and end of each span are not stated. Unverified.
