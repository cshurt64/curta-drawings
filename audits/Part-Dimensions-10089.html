The two-colour window ring

The functionally interesting feature of this part: a ring of roughly 18 rectangular window slots milled around the cap, alternating between weisses Segment (white segment — left unanodized, colourless) and schwarzes Segment gesandstrahlt (black segment, sand-blasted before black anodizing). A Segmentbegrenzung (segment boundary) line separates the two colours. This is the visual display the machine shows through the housing to indicate machine state (Inferred; the sheet does not name what the black/white indication means — that belongs in the theory-of-operation material, not this audit). The windows are individually positioned around the full circle in 20° increments with their own ±5′ tolerance (110°, 90°, 70°, 130°, and by the same spacing 10° through 350°); only a representative few are given their own guide markers here, since all ~18 are dimensioned the same way.

Two notes qualify the tolerancing: Toleranzen beziehen sich nur auf die Einfräsungen 2-H8 (the toleranced dimensions apply only to the milled 2 H8 recesses, not to the window openings in general) and Konzentrisch (the recesses must be concentric with the bore). A separate centring note, Mittigkeit des Fensters 2,6-50 zu Einfräsung 2-H8 = ±30 µ, ties the window recess to the milled slot within ±30 µm.
