  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved readings Unverified. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.

Compared with 10.104

Same outside diameter (Ø23.6), bore fit (Ø16 H6) and general family of angular/notch dimensions as 10.104, but this disc is 0.8 mm thick (against 10.104's 0.5 mm) and has no ground bevel — only the notch and its two corner radii. Read as a simpler locking disc in the same tens-carry mechanism, possibly a different stage or the mating disc without the ramp feature (Inferred; not stated on either sheet).
