The overall length (3.2 mm) is 0.2 mm more than the sum of the two printed lengths that should span the same distance (1 mm head height + 2 mm shank length = 3 mm): a reconciliation the sheet does not resolve, most likely because the dimensions are measured to slightly different reference points along the profile (Unverified — the same kind of gap seen on 10.093's 5 mm vs. 6 mm + 1.6 mm).

No diameter is given for the plain shoulder itself — the cylindrical land between the head (Ø2.5) and the necked/threaded portion (Ø0.9 reference). For a part named a shoulder screw, that land is normally the functional bearing/pivot surface, so its absence here is a genuine gap in the sheet, not an oversight in this reading (Unverified — see open points).

Open points

  1. The shoulder pin's own diameter is not dimensioned anywhere on the sheet (Unverified). Only the head (Ø2.5, raw stock) and the threaded portion's reference diameter (Ø0.9) are given; the plain land between them — the surface that would matter most if this pin were a rotating or sliding pivot in 10.245 — has no callout, tolerance, or fit. Worth checking 10.245's own assembly drawing or its mating part for whether a diameter is implied there instead.
  2. Overall length 3.2 mm vs. head 1 mm + shank 2 mm = 3 mm (Unverified). A 0.2 mm gap, most likely different reference faces, as seen before on 10.093.
  3. The 1× head-edge break's angle (Unverified). Printed as a bare "1×" with no angle digit visible; conventionally this would read "1×45°," but the angle itself is not confirmed from the sheet.
