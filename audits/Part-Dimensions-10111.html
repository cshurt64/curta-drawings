Finish: ▽, with ▽▽▽ in brackets. A note reads Diese Maße werden bei Abnahme besonders kontrolliert ("these dimensions are specially checked at acceptance"); no per-feature mark distinguishes which — read as applying to the sheet's toleranced fits and angles (Inferred).

Open points

  1. The disc's own diameter (10 mm) vs. the toothed-rim diameter (Ø23) are two distinct, unreconciled features (Confirmed as printed, relationship between them Unverified). The sheet does not show how a 10 mm disc body carries a 23 mm toothed rim in one continuous piece; likely the front view's "10 ±50" is an inner feature (e.g. a hub land) rather than the disc's outer edge, but this is not stated outright.
  2. The mechanism the toothed rim and slot serve (Unverified). This is the first part read in the zero-buffer group (pages 136+); its interaction is not yet established. Correction (2026-09-27): the original wording here guessed this involved the pawl 10.113, on the assumption that its page position (140, shortly after this part) meant it was part of the same group — but 10.113 turns out to be Kompl. Nr. 10.224 (Kompl. Sperrklinke, a base-assembly- stage-III mechanism per Machine-Facts.md §718), unrelated to the zero-buffer group 10.231/10.246 this disc belongs to. 10.114 (Nullpufferhebel, zero-buffer lever, per its own name — not yet read) remains a plausible mating part; page order alone should not be trusted to establish a functional relationship, as this case shows.
