The 10.017 Ø2.3 h7 shaft is in the ISO 286 over 1 through 3 mm step: current ISO IT7 = 10 µm, es = 0, ei = −10 µm, giving Ø2.290–2.300 mm (Derived). Its own 1949 fit table reads 0/−9 µm, an older-table limit of Ø2.291–2.300 mm (Confirmed). The 10.041 Ø2.3 H8 hole is in the same step: IT8 = 14 µm, EI = 0, ES = +14 µm, giving Ø2.300–2.314 mm (Derived, agreeing with its sheet). Their nominal cylindrical pairing would allow 0–24 µm diametral clearance using modern ISO values, or 0–23 µm with the sleeve sheet's h7 table (Derived). Swaging may change the final interface.

The 10.038 bore is an explicit non-ISO pre-finishing callout. Its Ø2.255–2.269 mm limits are 21–45 µm smaller than the 10.017 Ø2.290–2.300 mm ISO shaft limits (Derived). That numerical interference cannot be described as the finished fit: 10.017 calls for a tight sliding fit and 10.217-2 calls for finish broaching of bore and notch to a gauge. The post-broaching bore size and actual running clearance are Unverified. No radial clearance to the mating step-drum teeth is dimensioned on 10.217-2; 10.038's Ø4.400–4.450 mm outside envelope is inherited only.

Axial stack (Derived, not a direct assembly dimension)

On the 10.017 reduced section, the wheel and retaining sleeve have nominal thickness 0.5 + 1.8 = 2.3 mm against 2.7 mm available length. The 0.4 mm difference is an unallocated nominal remainder, not a specified running gap. With the child drawings' stated tolerances, the reduced section spans 2.6–2.8 mm and the two stacked parts span 2.23–2.37 mm; the arithmetic remainder spans 0.23–0.57 mm (worst-case limits, Derived). The drawing does not allocate that remainder among axial play, shoulder contact, swaged material or other features, so the functional axial clearance remains Unverified.
