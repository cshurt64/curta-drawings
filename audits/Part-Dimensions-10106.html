10.106-3 Locking Disc, Result Counter — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.106, Sperrscheibe für Resultat-Werk (locking disc for the result counter, RW — the counterpart of 10.104, the revolution-side locking disc), drawing 10.106-3, current; Ersatz für 10.106-2 (105.088), so it descends from the old-series drawing 105.088 through 10.106-2. Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 133 (viewer page 133). Scale 5:1 (reference view M1:1); stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm. Drawn 24.1.50; one change: index 2 in 3 (no. 203, 18.7.50). Read at 300 dpi on 2026-09-27.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.206 (complete tens bell); Stück p.Masch. 1, agreeing with the callout on p197 and BOM sheet 6 (p208).
  • Material: nichtrost. härtbarer Bandstahl hartgewalzt 0,5 ±10, Brin. 230–240 (Firma Matthey Neuveville) (stainless, hardenable strip steel, hard-rolled, 0.5 mm ±0.010, Brinell 230–240) — identical to 10.104's material.
  • General tolerance: ±100 µm linear, ±15′ angular. Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces; dashes for unmarked. No finish, surface or heat treatment stated (beyond the local geschliffen note).
  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved readings Unverified. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.

Compared with 10.104

Same outside diameter (Ø23.6), bore fit (Ø16 H6), thickness (0.5 mm), material and ground-bevel length (11.35 ±20 mm) as 10.104. The angular layout differs (114°45′ and 32°45′ here against 10.104's 146° and 103°30′), placing the notch and bevel at different positions appropriate to the result side's own carry timing — the same UW/RW pairing pattern already seen between 10.100/10.101.
