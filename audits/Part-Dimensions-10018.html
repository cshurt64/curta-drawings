10.018-5 Carry-Slider Bearing — dimensional audit and 3× reference

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.018, Zehnerschieberlager (carry-slider bearing), drawing revision 10.018-5, current; it supersedes 10.018-4. Identity verified against Drawing-Index.md: page 30 is 10.018-5, and the sheet's own title block agrees. Scale 10:1; the sheet is stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm.
  • Primary source: original Contina engineering drawing, drawing-set PDF page 30 / drawing-viewer page 30, rasterized at 300–400 dpi.
  • Parent(s) and quantity: 10.234 Base assembly, stage III; 15 per machine; Stück 15.
  • Material: Brass Ms 58 Pb. General tolerance: ±100 µm; angles none. Runout: none (dashes). Drawn: 25.10.1950. Revisions: index 4→5 (change 134, 25.10.50).

Derived clearances with the sliders

The bearing's slider slot (view A) is 2 +0.050/+0.020 wide and 0.5 +0.050/+0.020 high; the sliders 10.005/10.006 are 2 ±0.010 wide (tab) and 0.5 ±0.010 thick. Pairing the two sheets (Inferred by nominal size; no sheet names the mate; Machine-Facts records the slider running in its bearing):

Direction Slot Slider Clearance
Width 2.020–2.050 mm 1.990–2.010 mm 10 to 60 µm
Thickness 0.520–0.550 mm 0.490–0.510 mm 10 to 60 µm

Both are Derived from explicit limits on the two sheets, not ISO codes. The identical 10–60 µm band in both directions looks deliberate. Note the bearing's own 2 ±50 tongue is centered to the slot within ±50 µm.
