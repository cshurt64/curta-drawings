ISO 286 fit cases

ISO 286-1 defines the system; ISO 286-2 tabulates the deviations. Both cases lie in the "over 1 through 3 mm" step.

Feature Case IT / fundamental deviation ISO limits Sheet fit table Status
Upper pivot hole 2 H11 IT11 = 60 µm; EI = 0 2.000–2.060 mm +60/0 Confirmed; agrees
Lower pivot hole 2 F8 IT8 = 14 µm; EI = +6 µm (F) 2.006–2.020 mm +21/+7 Confirmed callout; the sheet is 1 µm above ISO on both limits (Unverified)

For H, EI = 0 and the zone is above nominal; for F the whole zone is above nominal, leaving a clearance for a pin. The 1 µm offset on F8 is the same one seen on 10.010's 1.8 F8.

Open points

  1. The slot dimensions (Unverified). 2.5, 1.2, r0.8 and r3 around the arm slot are not tied to labelled faces.
  2. The two pivot holes (Inferred). 2 H11 (upper) and 2 F8 (lower): the lower is a close sliding fit for a pivot pin, the upper a wider, general hole; the sheet does not say what passes through each.
  3. 2 F8 reads +21/+7 (Unverified). ISO 286-2 gives +20/+6.
  4. The dark dashed arcs. The heavy dot-dash edges on the arm mark the portion nicht runden (not to be rounded).
