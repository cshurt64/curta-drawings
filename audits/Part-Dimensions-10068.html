ISO 286 fit cases

ISO 286-1 defines the system; ISO 286-2 tabulates the deviations. One case, in the "over 1 through 3 mm" step.

Feature Case IT / fundamental deviation ISO limits Sheet fit table Status
Outer diameter Ø3 R8 IT8 = 14 µm; R8: es = −10 µm, ei = −23 µm 2.977–2.990 mm −10/−24 Confirmed callout; the sheet is 1 µm wider at the lower limit (Unverified)

R (uppercase, a shaft fundamental deviation despite the capital letter on this drawing family) puts the whole zone below nominal with a small interference allowance built in. This is the same 1 µm-wide pattern noted elsewhere on 1–3 mm parts (10.009, 10.013, 10.020, 10.032, 10.063), though there it was IT7; here it is IT8 with an R deviation, so it may be a separate reading rather than the same house-table effect.

Open points

  1. Ø3 R8 reads −10/−24 (Unverified). ISO 286-2 gives −10/−23 for R8 at 1–3 mm: the sheet's zone is 1 µm wider.
  2. Naming. The drawing's own title is Umsteuerungsring ("reversing ring" if translated literally); the service manual's factory English is the step drum positioning ring. Both point to the same drum-positioning function (Confirmed per Machine-Facts §2a), not the quotient-side reversing-lever family.
