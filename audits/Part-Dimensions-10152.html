10.152-2 Spring Ring — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.152, Federring (spring ring / split circlip), drawing 10.152-2, current; Ersatz für 10.152-1 (superseded revision, page 186, not read in detail — the current revision supersedes it per change 150). Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set and catalog page 187 (viewer page 187). Scale 10:1 (reference view M1:1); stamped Abmaße in µ = 1/1000 mm as a printed title-block row on this sheet layout, rather than the large diagonal handwritten stamp used on the pin sheets. Drawn 7.11.1950; change block: change 150, "Index 1 in 2 geändert" (index 1 changed to 2, not stated what changed). Read at 220 dpi on 2026-09-29.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.231 (complete bearing plate, Kompl. Lagerplatte); Stück p.Masch. 1. Already Confirmed in Assembly-Links.csv (10.231,10.152,1), corroborated by the leader on assembly drawing p238 and BOM sheet 8 (p210, 10'152 Federring nichtr. Federstahl 1).
  • Material: nichtrostender Federstahl (rustproof spring steel). Form: a split ring (radial gap ≥60° minimum free angle), not a machined part — no heat-treatment note, no surface-finish symbol, no surface-treatment note (all three dashes), consistent with a formed spring part rather than a turned one.
  • General tolerance: a dash on this sheet — every dimension carries its own explicit tolerance instead (the Max. Abweichung nicht tolerierter Maße field itself reads ± — µ). Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces; unmarked values both dashes.
  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved readings Unverified. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.

ISO 286 fit case

None. Every dimension on this sheet carries an explicit ± or +/− tolerance rather than an ISO letter-code fit; there is no H/h/p-style callout to check against ISO 286-2.
