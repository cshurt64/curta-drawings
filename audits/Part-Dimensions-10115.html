Load/deflection graph

The sheet carries a small load-vs-something graph, distinct in form from the two coil springs audited so far (10.073, 10.086), which each gave two explicit load values at two named deflections. Here the graph shows:

  • Two reference lines from the origin at 47°30′ and 40° (printed angles, read as-is).
  • Two load values on one axis: 0.66 kg and 0.79 kg.
  • One diagonal line, presumably the spring's own load/deflection curve, crossing the two reference lines.

Unverified. The deflection value(s) paired with the two loads are not legible or derivable from this rendering, and the two angled reference lines' exact graphical role (a nomograph-style min/max spring-rate check, most likely) is not stated in words anywhere on the sheet. Unlike 10.073/10.086, this graph cannot be reduced to a two-point spring-rate estimate from the sheet alone — flagging rather than guessing, consistent with those two open items.
