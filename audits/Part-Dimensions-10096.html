The revolution-counter tooth grid (Inferred)

Both revolution-counter segments dimension a 90° arc: 10.096 spans its nine teeth, and 10.091 has a 90° blank in front of its single tooth. Read together they give a 10° tooth pitch (9 × 10° = 90°), unlike the result-counter segments' 11°15′. That is Inferred: the sheets dimension only the arc, and the pitch is not printed. It also breaks the simple "made from 10.076" picture, since a segment cut from the master blank would inherit the 11°15′ pitch; the note on the sheet says Aus 10.076-3 angefertigt, so either the master blank was reworked for a different pitch or the 90° measures something other than the tooth span (Unverified). Drum-Assembly-10213.md §3 records the shared 90° envelope but not the pitch.

Plate thickness conflicts with the current master

This sheet's material line reads 0,5 −0/−20: 0.480–0.500 mm. The current master 10.076-4 reads 0,5 −20/−40: 0.460–0.480 mm. The master's revision table records Index 3 in 4 geändert, change 139, 26.10.50; every derived sheet is dated 13.1.50 or 17.1.50, cites Aus 10.076-3 angefertigt and shows an empty revision table. The likeliest reading is that change 139 shifted the thickness tolerance and the derived sheets were not reissued (Inferred; no sheet states what change 139 altered). The two ranges do not overlap except at 0.480 mm. It matters because the drum stack in Drum-Assembly-10213.md §5 builds the axial stations from 0.5 mm plates; check the station arithmetic against both ranges before adopting either. The parts list agrees with the derived sheets: 10.210 sheets 4 and 5 give all twelve segments (the master included) as 0.5 −0.02/0 or +0/−0.02, so 10.076-4 is the outlier; with −0.020/−0.040 the 37-plate stack could not reach the stations on 10.213-2 (see Part-Dimensions-10213.md).

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size; a scaled nominal exactly on a size-step boundary belongs to the lower step.
