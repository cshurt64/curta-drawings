10.038-3 Drive Wheel I — dimensional audit and 3× reference

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.038, Schaltrad I (Drive Wheel I), drawing revision 10.038-3
  • Primary source: original Contina engineering drawing, drawing-set PDF page 54 / drawing-viewer page 54
  • Drawing values: Confirmed from the current drawing and title block
  • Calculated limits: Derived from the drawing's explicit micron deviations
  • 3× values: Derived geometric references, not adopted fabrication tolerances or factory specifications

The bore is the one ISO letter fit on this sheet (* 2,3-J8, fit table 2,3-J8 ± 7); the other tolerances are explicit micron deviations. The 3× table below multiplies both geometry and tolerance magnitudes by three so the result is mathematically unambiguous; the project still needs to decide which tolerances should instead be retained or replaced with manufacturing-process targets.

The Ø2.3 entry is a J8 bore whose fit-table entry gives a symmetric ±7 µm, which is JS8 (IT8 = 14 µm at this size); the same entry appears on 10.049 and 10.053. As on 10.049, the letter and the tabulated numbers are not the same ISO zone and the numbers govern. An earlier version of this audit read the entry as a −38 µm mean deviation with ±7 µm (−45/−31 µm); that reading does not match the current sheet and was corrected on 2026-10-10.

The drawing specifies 20 pieces per machine. It calls for high-quality hardenable 0.5 ±0.020 mm strip steel, hardened to Brinell 230–240.

Names for the edges of one tooth

One tooth is three edges and a corner. The two open ends join the neighbouring teeth, and there is no fourth closing edge. The edge letters A–C below are not the assumption tags A1–A10.

Name What it is Parameter Status

"Tip arc" was considered for Edge B and rejected, because tooth_tip_radius is the radius of Edge C and the two names would collide.

Assumptions made in the FreeCAD model

The author's learning FreeCAD model of this part builds some geometry that no callout on the scan states. These are the author's and the assistant's readings and modelling choices, listed so that none of them is mistaken for a drawing fact. The same tags [A1]–[A10] are on the 3× reference sheet. Inferred = a reading of the scan; Decision = a modelling choice; Unverified = not settled.

Tag Assumption Basis Status
A1 Each straight flank is tangent to the Ø2.8 reference ring at 0°, ±72°, ±144°; the model builds the 0° flank as a vertical line from (4.2, 0) at 3×. The scan prints no tangent or vertical callout. Measured at 400 dpi (2026-10-11): flank 1.42 mm from the centre against 1.400 mm for the Ø2.8 ring (0.02 mm, within hand-drafting accuracy at 20:1), 0.4° off the image vertical (not separable from scan skew); the traced gap bottoms read Ø2.83 Inferred

A3 The 0,4 end facet is an arc on the envelope circle, arc length 0.4 mm (1.2 mm at 3×). A straight tangent flat would differ by about 9 µm at 1× (27 µm at 3×); the scan cannot tell them apart. A8 The other four teeth are exact 72° copies of the first. Built 2026-10-11 with the Sketcher polar transform; each copy is tied to the original by Equal constraints and by s_-bound tip-length and flank-direction expressions. Overlaid on the 300 dpi trace of the scan (421 points, 1× trace good to about 0.03 mm), all five cycles lie within 0.17 mm of the model at 3× (rms 0.04–0.09 mm, mean offset 0.01 ± 0.02 mm): about the accuracy of the trace, and comparable to the ±30′ the drawing allows lug to tooth (0.06 mm at the tip at 3×). Identical by construction, supported by the overlay, not measured on a part Inferred (supported)
A9 Edge C (the R0.75 curved flank) has its centre on the Ø3.3 circle. Decision (author, 2026-10-11), applied in the model the same day. The saved model (read 14:34) puts all five centres 4.950 mm from the axis at 3× (exactly Ø9.9); R2.25 and the corner on the Ø2.8 ring drive the arc. The valley corners lie at Ø2.8, so the name "tooth-root reference diameter" for Ø3.3 is doubtful. The earlier consistency check was weak: the drawing's own tolerances on the inputs (facet width ±50 µm, corner ring +0/−100 µm, envelope +50/0 µm, arc against flat) move the centre radius from 1.619 to 1.735 mm, 116 µm against the 40 µm band; only 10 of 32 corner combinations land inside it. Adding the decision over-constrained the arc, so the end-facet width (0,4, general ±50 µm, Konstruktionsmass) and its four copies became reference values, which read 0.384 mm at 1× (1.152 mm at 3×, −16 µm, inside its band). Releasing the radius instead has no solution at facet 0.400 and swings R by 100 µm for a 30 µm facet change. Saved model: 0 degrees of freedom, no conflicts, 1 valid solid, volume 82.366 mm³ (−0.41 %), scale 2 and 6 pass. Against the traced scan the overlay is no better and no worse (max 0.17 mm at 3× against 0.14) Decision, not a drawing fact
A10 facet_ray_angle (9°) is kept as a parameter only; what the ray constructs is not known. A reconstruction of the upper-left facet from the A1–A3 geometry puts its ends at about 20° and 33° from the apex (15° and 25° from the centre), so the ray is not simply the direction to a facet end Unverified

Assembly relevance

The title block lists complete assemblies 10.217, 10.218, 10.219 and 10.230. In those assemblies, wheel thickness contributes to the axial stack, the bore fit controls the sleeve interface, and the tooth/root envelope controls drive engagement. Those are inherited component dimensions, not direct assembly dimensions.
