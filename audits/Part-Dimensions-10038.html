Unlike 10.017 and 10.041, this sheet does not assign ISO 286 letter/grade fits. Its tolerances are explicit micron deviations. The 3× table below multiplies both geometry and tolerance magnitudes by three so the result is mathematically unambiguous; the project still needs to decide which tolerances should instead be retained or replaced with manufacturing-process targets.

The drawing specifies 20 pieces per machine. It calls for high-quality hardenable 0.5 ±0.020 mm strip steel, hardened to Brinell 230–240.

Assembly relevance

The title block lists complete assemblies 10.217, 10.218, 10.219 and 10.230. In those assemblies, wheel thickness contributes to the axial stack, the bore fit controls the sleeve interface, and the tooth/root envelope controls drive engagement. Those are inherited component dimensions, not direct assembly dimensions.
