Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.046, Kommaschieber (decimal-point slider), drawing 10.046-3, current; Ersatz für 10.046 (the earlier drawing carried no revision index). The sheet's own count is a fourth corroboration.
  • Material: Anticorodal B (a hardenable aluminium alloy), circled a; farblos eloxiert (colourless anodized). No heat treatment.
  • General tolerance: ±0.05 mm linear; angular a dash. Runout: ±0.01 mm on marked surfaces (10 µm, half the usual 20 µm); dashes for unmarked. Finish: ▽▽▽. The circled a marks dimensions besonders kontrolliert (specially checked on acceptance).
