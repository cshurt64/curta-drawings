10.001-10 Step Drum Body — dimensional audit and 3× reference

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.001, Stufenwalzenkörper (step drum body), drawing 10.001-10, current; replaces 10.001-9. Identity verified against Drawing-Index.md: page 2 is 10.001-10, and the sheet's own title block agrees. Scale 5:1 and 20:1 (detail C); stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm.
  • Primary source: original Contina engineering drawing, drawing-set PDF page 2 / drawing-viewer page 2, rasterized at 400–700 dpi.
  • Parent(s) and quantity: 10.213 Complete Step Drum; 1 per machine. Material: Decoltal Ø21 bar; anodized, hard, colourless. General tolerance: ±100 µm, ±1°. Runout: 20 µm marked radial and side; 50 µm unmarked. Drawn: 14.10.48. Revisions: index 7→8 (change 208, 2.8.50), 8→9 (233, 29.8.50), 9→10 (247, 27.10.50).
  • Labels: Confirmed (printed), Derived (calculated), Unverified, Inferred; 3× values are Derived project references, not factory specifications.
  • Form of the part (reading, Unverified): the body is turned from the Ø21 bar and then milled. The 1:1 inset at the top left of the sheet shows the finished silhouette: most of the Ø18.4 shoulder is milled away, which is why Sections A–A (vertical centre line) and B–B (horizontal centre line) show different outlines. Neither half of B–B shows the whole turned blank.

Official standards references: ISO 286-1 and ISO 286-2.

ISO 286 fit cases

For h, es = 0 and the zone lies below nominal; for capital letters (holes) the zone is above or around nominal per the fundamental deviation.

Where the U8 applies (reading, Unverified). In Section B–B the 3 Ø U8 extension lines bound an unhatched band about 6.3–6.4 mm from the axis that runs through the stub and the flange; they do not reach the central bore. The right end view has a circle of about Ø3 (by scale) on the horizontal centre line at 6,4 ±50. The central bore's lines run unbroken from the stub end to the hub-end counterbore, with the single J8 callout. An earlier version of this audit put the U8 on a flange zone of the central bore; that reading is withdrawn.

Fits with Ø3 h7 parts (Derived, from two sheets)

The main axle 10.003 is Ø3 h7 (0/−9 µm on its sheet; ISO −10), and so is the driving pin 10.072. Assembly drawing 10.213-2 draws the 10.072 pin on the drum axis in both side views.

Pair Hole Shaft Fit
A Ø3 h7 part in the central J8 bore 2.993–3.007 2.991–3.000 clearance to +16 µm, interference to 7 µm: a transition fit
A Ø3 h7 part in the off-axis U8 hole 2.968–2.982 2.991–3.000 interference 9 to 32 µm: a press fit

Both Ø3 h7 parts on the axis (10.003 and 10.072) can only sit in the central J8 bore; which length of the bore each occupies is not stated on either sheet (Inferred). What sits in the off-axis U8 hole, and whether the dashed Ø1.7 and M1,7 belong to a part pressed into it, is Unverified.

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size; a scaled nominal exactly on a size-step boundary belongs to the lower step.
