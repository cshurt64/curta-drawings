10.001-10 Step Drum Body — dimensional audit and 3× reference

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.001, Stufenwalzenkörper (step drum body), drawing 10.001-10, current; replaces 10.001-9. Identity verified against Drawing-Index.md: page 2 is 10.001-10, and the sheet's own title block agrees. Scale 5:1 and 20:1 (detail C); stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm.

  • Parent(s) and quantity: 10.213 Complete Step Drum; 1 per machine. Material: Decoltal Ø21 bar; anodized, hard, colourless. General tolerance: ±100 µm, ±1°. Runout: 20 µm marked radial and side; 50 µm unmarked. Drawn: 14.10.48. Revisions: index 7→8 (change 208, 2.8.50), 8→9 (233, 29.8.50), 9→10 (247, 27.10.50).

Official standards references: ISO 286-1 and ISO 286-2.

Fits with the main axle 10.003 (Derived, from two sheets)

The drum is bored Ø3 in two zones: J8 (±7 µm sheet; JS8) for the running section and U8 (−18/−32 µm) at the flange. The axle is Ø3 h7 (0/−9 µm on its sheet; ISO −10):

Pair Hole Shaft Fit
10.003 in the J8 bore 2.993–3.007 2.991–3.000 clearance to +16 µm, interference to 7 µm: a transition fit
10.003 in the U8 bore 2.968–2.982 2.991–3.000 interference 9 to 32 µm: a press fit

The pairing is Inferred by nominal size and the two bore zones on this sheet; no sheet says which section of the axle sits in which bore. The driving pin 10.072 is also Ø3 h7 and stands on the drum axis (10.213-2), so it competes for one of the same bores; see Part-Dimensions-10213.md. It suggests the drum is located and driven by a pressed seat at the flange and guided by a sliding seat along its length. Machine-Facts §4a calls the pair "F1 / F3 ... J8 running"; the transition fit at the J8 zone is not a clear running fit.

Machining notes: Toleranz der Segmente nach entsprechender Lehre (segment tolerance by the corresponding gauge) is the unrecoverable gauge already recorded in Machine-Facts §3; the axial stations of the segments live on 10.213-2, not here.

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size; a scaled nominal exactly on a size-step boundary belongs to the lower step.
