The result plates total 1 + 5 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 6 + 2 + 5 = 37; with 7 revolution plates that is 44 tooth segments (Derived; all counts also match the BOM).

10.048 is not an M1.4 screw. Its own sheet gives Ø3.5 ±0.100 head, M1.7 thread, 3 ±0.100 length, brass Ms 58 Pb. The three M1.4 × 3 screws are the standard VSM 12124 item, a separate fastener. Machine-Facts had merged them ("M1.4×3 cylinder screws (10.048, VSM 12124)"); it is corrected. The plan view supports the split: two solid slotted heads about Ø3.5 seen from above, and three smaller dashed circles for heads hidden underneath (Inferred from size and position). The BOM lists 7 VSM 12124 M1.4 × 3 screws in all, "for retaining plate, step drum"; 3 are here, and the other 4 are not yet reconciled (10.234 records one leader).

Fits with the body's Ø3 bores (Derived)

The pin is Ø3 h7 (2.991–3.000 mm on the sheet's −9). 10.001 has a Ø3 J8 bore (2.993–3.007) and a Ø3 U8 bore (2.968–2.982); see Part-Dimensions-10001.md. The pin in the J8 bore is a transition fit (clearance up to 16 µm, interference up to 7 µm); in the U8 bore it is a press fit (interference 9–32 µm). The sheet does not say which zone holds the pin, and the main axle 10.003 is also Ø3 h7, so the pairing stays Unverified. A pin that stays put while sticking 7.5 mm out of the top suggests the U8 press seat, which is only an inference.

What the arithmetic says (Derived). With plates at the sheets' 0.5 −0/−0.020, an as-rolled stack falls inside a ±0.050 station band only if the plates run within about 0.050/n of the upper limit (35 plates would have to average at least 0.4986 mm). With the master's −0.020/−0.040 the stack never reaches any station. Neither range lets the stations be held by stacking rolled plates alone, so the tolerance must be met another way; the note on 10.001, "Toleranz der Segmente nach entsprechender Lehre" (segment tolerance by the corresponding gauge), points to gauge selection (Inferred; the gauge is on no sheet). The parts list (10.210 sheets 4 and 5) gives all twelve segments as 0.5 −0.02/0 or +0/−0.02, agreeing with the eleven derived sheets, so the master's −0.020/−0.040 is the outlier. Both are recorded in Machine-Facts.md §2.

The 37-plate height (18.5 mm) equals the 18.5 ±0.020 shoulder length on 10.001 at nominal (Derived). Which body feature that length bounds, and how 10.119 fits relative to it, are Unverified.
