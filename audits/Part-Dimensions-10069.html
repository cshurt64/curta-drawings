10.069-3 Sliding Ring, Main Axle — key measurements and tolerances

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.069. This is a corrected identity (2026-09-26): Drawing-Index.md and the generated catalog previously gave 10.069's German name as Schwerspannstift (a standard heavy-duty spring pin), but the sheet's own title block reads Gleitring zu Hauptachse (sliding ring for the main axle). The Schwerspannstift S1×5,5 standard part is unrelated: it is a separate standard fastener called out on 10.223's own sheet and already recorded in Part-Dimensions-10223.md and Assembly-Links.csv. Drawing-Index.md and Machine-Facts.md §4 have been corrected in the same edit as this audit.
  • Drawing 10.069-3, current; Ersatz für 10.069-2. Identity agrees with the corrected Drawing-Index.md row and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 90 (viewer page 90). Scale 10:1 (reference view M 1:1); stamped Abmaße in μ = 1/1000 mm. Drawn 24.2.50; the change block is empty. Read at 300 dpi on 2026-09-26.
  • Parent and quantity: Kompl. Nr. 10.223 (complete main axle); Stück p.Masch. 2: two per machine. There is no leader for this part on 10.223's own sheet (10.223-5, p228), so the where-used edge rests on this sheet's own Kompl. Nr. and count only (Inferred) — see the open points.
  • Material: RRMD Ø6 nichtrost. (stainless steel, RRMD grade, Ø6 mm bar stock). No surface or heat treatment stated.
  • General tolerance: ±100 µm linear; angular a dash. Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces, 100 µm radial on unmarked (side runout on unmarked: a dash). Finish: ▽▽, with ▽▽▽ in brackets.
