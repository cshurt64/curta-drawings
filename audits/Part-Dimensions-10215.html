The slider allocation closes

The slider, its spring and its ball are each 8 per machine (BOM sheets 3 and 9), and the housing has eight slider positions (confirmed by the user, 2026-09-24), so all eight sit under 10.215; the M10:1 detail on this sheet shows one of them, as one leader stands for several pieces. The slider drawn on the clearing cap 10.244 is one of the same eight, so it is not counted again there. Machine-Facts §4 recorded 10.046 as 7 against 8 on the BOM; the BOM's 8 is read again at 300 dpi and §4 is corrected.
