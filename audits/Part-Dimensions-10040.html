10.040-5 Counter Body — dimensional audit and 3× reference

Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.040, Zählwerkskörper (counter body), drawing revision 10.040-5, current; it supersedes 10.040-1. Identity verified against Drawing-Index.md: page 56 is 10.040‑5 Zählwerkskörper, and the sheet's own title block agrees. The body's axial dimensions are all measured from the two end faces, so its own stack closes (6.7 = 5.6 + 1.1). It cannot be tied to the axial steps on 10.226-3 (1.3 ±100, 1.5 ±100, 1.3 ±20, 4 ±20) without a shared datum that neither sheet prints, so the axial stack of the assembly stays open.

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted. Where a callout carries an ISO code, it is re-applied at the scaled basic size and the limits recalculated. A 3× table is not a factory specification, and the sheet's sub-3 mm zone widths and general tolerances are not silently carried over.

Ø6 sits exactly on the upper boundary of the "over 3 through 6 mm" ISO size step, so it belongs to that step; Ø6.001 would fall in the next step. The 3× M7 and E8 values are for that reading. Ø36, Ø60 and Ø81 fall inside their steps.

The 3× axial height (20.1 mm) and flange diameter (Ø97.8) are large for a printed part; per the project's tolerance decision the printed prototype fits come from measurement, and none of this is a fabrication tolerance.
