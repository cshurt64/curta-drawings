Relation to OBS-007 and a project decision

The notch pair on this sheet (Zacken: two 90° V-notches with R0.2 roots, 1.4 mm wide and 0.6 mm deep, one on each edge, absolut gratfrei) is the tooth-and-valley that the 10.007 spring legs reach (Inferred from the OBS-007 evidence, not stated on any sheet). It is 4.6 mm from the slider's shoulder, so 13.8 mm at 3×.

a project decision records a caliper span of ~17.25 mm on the installed printed slider, from the spring's mounting slot to the tooth-and-valley; ÷ 3 is 5.75 mm. No dimension on this sheet is identifiable as that span. The only nearby figure is the side view's 5.8 ±100 (×3 = 17.4 mm, band 17.1–17.7 mm), which runs along the formed arm between two bends, a different feature by my reading. The numerical closeness is a lead to check, not a confirmation of a project decision (Unverified). Two things the sheet does say bear on the printed replica: the notches must be absolutely burr-free, and the whole blank is toleranced ±20–50 µm, so a 3× printed slider's notch geometry is set by the print, not by this table.
