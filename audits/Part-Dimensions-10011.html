Scan-legibility note (Unverified, applies to the whole table except row 3). The fit table's deviation column is written in very small handwriting and the scan does not resolve it cleanly even at 900 dpi (re-rendering above 600 dpi added no new legible pixels — the ceiling is in the original scan, not the render). Row 3 (43-h8, 0/−39) is printed large and clear enough to read with confidence, and it agrees with the Derived ISO value exactly, which is the same kind of confirmation the project has found on other sheets (e.g. 10.148, 10.150). Rows 2, 4, 7, 8 and 9 are partly legible and consistent with the Derived values as far as read; rows 5, 6 and 10 could not be confirmed independently; row 1 is the one outlier where the legible digits (+31/+7) do not match the Derived +53/+20 — flagged, not resolved (see Open point 1). Unlike the project's recurring "1 µm narrower than ISO" pattern seen on many 1–3 mm sheets (e.g. 10.009, 10.072, 10.013), a 22/13 µm gap on row 1 is too large to be that same house-table effect; it is more likely a misread digit than a real factory deviation, but this is not confirmed.

Open points

  1. Fit-table row 1, Ø25 F8 (Needs you / Unverified). The legible sheet digits (+31/+7) do not match the Derived ISO 286-2 value (+53/+20) for F8 at 18–30 mm. This is a bigger gap than the project's usual "1 µm narrower than ISO" house-table pattern, so it reads as a misread digit rather than a genuine factory deviation — but this is not confirmed. A look at the original sheet (not the scan) would settle it.
  2. Angular fan, exact count and spacing (Open). Several of the front- and rear-face angle labels (120°, 125°±25', 160°, 230° on the front face; 120° on the rear face) are tightly spaced or carry unusual tolerance bands on the scan and could not be independently confirmed against the drawn slot positions. Whether the printed sequence is a single monotonic sweep from one datum or several independent measured positions is not stated.
  3. Boss detail M1 / 2 / 4 (Open). The small thread callout beside two bare numerals (2, 4) near the top of Section A–A is transcribed as printed; neither the sheet nor any assembly drawing read so far explains what the numerals reference.
  4. Base-flange diameter Ø4 ±200 (Open). The leading digit is not reliably legible; transcribed as read.
  5. Section C–C's 1:25-style callout (Open). Read as a taper or angular ratio; the sheet gives no further label and it was not cross-checked against any other drawing.
  6. 5.6 +?/0 axial dimension (Open). The nominal (5.6) is legible; the exact upper deviation is not.
