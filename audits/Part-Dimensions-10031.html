The grades overlap: with a tolerance of +0.04/−0.02 each grade is 0.06 mm wide but the grades are only 0.02 mm apart, so a ring can belong to up to three grades. Read together with the sorting note, this suggests the rings are measured and sorted into the nearest 0.02 mm and then chosen (selective fit) rather than made to one of four toleranced sizes (Inferred).
