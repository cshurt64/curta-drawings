Axle and star: the two 4 % tapers (Inferred)

This axle ends in a 4 % taper 4.3 ±0.400 mm long, and the finished star's blank (10.052-4, Part-Dimensions-10052.md) has a Ø2 0/−0.020 bore with a 4 % taper. Two matching 4 % tapers suggest the star is pressed onto the tapered end of the axle as a cone fit, and the Richtmass Ø1.96 (0.04 below Ø2) is consistent with a 4 % taper 1 mm along the cone; if 4 % means 0.04 mm of diameter per mm of length, the small end would be about Ø1.83 mm (Derived under that reading; the percentage definition is Unverified). No sheet states the pairing.

The 90° V-groove along the axle is the key that the ratchet wheels' and drive wheels' 90° notches ('nose') ride in; its centering (±20 µm) and its own taper (0.1 ‰) are tighter than anything else on the sheet. The revision history has two entries (indices 5→6 and 6→7); the index-7 change of 18.9.50 is not identified on the sheet.

Machine-Facts §4 listed this axle as Stainless Ø2 h7, which the sheet confirms (Ø2-h7 profiliert), so no correction is needed there.
