The 23.4 h7 fit (ISO 286-1 system, ISO 286-2 deviations) — recalculated

Basic size 23.4 mm lies in the 18–30 mm step. IT7 in that step is 21 µm; for h, es = 0 and the zone lies below nominal, so the limits are Ø23.379–23.400 mm. This matches the sheet's own fit-table entry 0 / −21 exactly, so the recalculation agrees with the drawing. Confirmed entry, Derived limits. What the diameter belongs to is not stated: the elevation and the plan view scale it to the widest disc of the stack (400 px on a 17.1 px/mm scan, i.e. 23.4 mm), which is consistent with the Ø23.6 ±50 of the four locking discs but not identical to it (Inferred; see the open points).

Open points

  1. 10.009 material (Confirmed, and a correction). Machine-Facts §4 recorded Anticorodal Ø20; both BOM sheet 1 (p201) and the sheet's own Werkstoff field read Decoltal Ø20. Corrected in Machine-Facts.
  2. 3-H7 on 10.009-7 (Unverified). The fit table appears to read +9 / 0; ISO IT7 for a 1–3 mm step is 10 µm. To be settled in 10.009's own audit, not here.
  3. The 0.35 mm stack gap (Unverified). See above.
  4. What the 23.4 h7 belongs to (Inferred). The sheet does not name it.
  5. Height planes (Unverified). Only the 3.4 dimension is tied to a plane (flange top to the underside of the first two discs, by arithmetic).
  6. Transitional design note (Confirmed, meaning Unverified). 10.009-7 carries a note that the slot and the 60° countersink are a transitional design "until tooth segments with the large pitch and the old locking discs are used up" — i.e. two generations of parts coexisted; which is drawn here is not stated.
