Fit with the setting axle 10.061 (Derived)

The bore is Ø2.5 −0.009/−0.023 mm (2.477–2.491) and the setting axle 10.061 is Ø2.5 h6 (0/−7 µm on its sheet; ISO −6): 2.493–2.500. Both parts are listed on the complete setting axle 10.212 (Confirmed), so the pairing is supported by the assembly, though the interface is not printed:

Quantity Calculation Value
Minimum interference axle min − bore max = 2.493 − 2.491 2 µm
Maximum interference axle max − bore min = 2.500 − 2.477 23 µm
Character interference in every case a light press fit

So the digit roller is pressed onto the axle rather than rotating on it, and turning the axle turns the digit. The explicit −9/−23 zone is the same width as an IT8 zone (14 µm) shifted below nominal; it is not a standard letter fit.

Notes

Eight rollers per machine, one per setting slider position. The sheet shows no digit engraving; the numbers on the setting display are a separate feature of the setting axle assembly (10.212) or the housing (10.002), not dimensioned here. Machine-Facts §4 already gives the material as Anticorodal Ø6.5; the sheet and BOM agree.

Project 3× reference

Convention used here: the project's tolerance strategy (a project decision) ties tolerance to the manufacturing process, not to the 3× factor. "Simply scaled" values are a comparison only and are not adopted, general tolerances are not silently carried over, and a 3× table is not a factory specification. ISO codes are re-applied at the scaled size; a scaled nominal exactly on a size-step boundary belongs to the lower step.
