The sheet's zone is 9 µm wide, not 10. With 10.009's 3 H7 (+9/0) and 10.072's 3 h7 (0/−9), that makes three sheets where an IT7 at 3 mm is 9 µm wide, which points at the tolerance table the factory used, not at three separate slips. That is a reading, not a finding.

  3. ~10.5 (Confirmed, meaning approximate). The only dimension printed with a tilde and no tolerance.
  4. A left-hand thread. M1.7 links is a left-hand M1.7. The mating part is read as the crank pin 10.022, whose threaded end is M1.7 linksgängig (Part-Dimensions-10022.md); the sheet itself does not say so (Inferred).
