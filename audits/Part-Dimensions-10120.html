Selective-fit grading (this sheet's fit control)

No ISO 286 letter fit is used. Instead the sheet grades one dimension, labelled a, into eight classes in 5 µm steps, with a note aussortiert nach Pos. ("sorted by position") linking the grade table to the callout:

Overall range 14.480–14.520 mm across the eight grades. Per Machine-Facts.md §698, the disc so graded is selected to give an interference fit under a swaged rim in the assembled 10.206 — this is the same kind of selective-fit grading used on 10.064, 10.077 and 10.099 elsewhere in this drawing set.
