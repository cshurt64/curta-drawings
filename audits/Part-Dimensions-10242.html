An instruction drawing, not an assembly with a parts count. Component callouts and factory count

The leaders are not entered as children of 10.242. Each named part belongs to its own parent assembly, where its quantity is recorded, and 10.242 has no defined piece count, so a parent edge here would double-count it. The interactive guide names every leader (110 part numbers and 17 standard-part labels) using the catalog names; quantities and parents are on each part's own sheet and in Where-Used.md.
