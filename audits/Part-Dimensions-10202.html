Fits and stacks derived from those figures

  • Ø16 H7 bore (*, runout-controlled). 16 mm lies in the 10–18 mm step: IT7 = 18 µm, EI = 0, zone above nominal ⇒ Ø16.000–16.018 (Derived; the sheet prints no table entry to compare with here).
  • Screw head in its counterbore (Inferred). Ø3 head in the Ø3.2 ±50 counterbore leaves 0.10–0.30 mm of diametral clearance (both ±0.05 by the general tolerance). The head, 0.7 ±50, against a 0.8 ±50 pocket puts the head 0.0–0.2 mm below flush. Both readings assume that the Ø3.2 and the 0.8 belong to the pocket the head sits in; the seat is drawn at 45° on 10.030-7 and the meaning of the 2.8 is not stated (Unverified).
