At the 1× nominal, the axial parts on the 4.7 mm reduced 10.032 section sum to 1.0 + 1.5 + 1.8 = 4.3 mm, leaving 0.4 mm unallocated (Derived). The sheet does not dimension the axial clearances or tell which faces consume that remainder; no closed axial fit is asserted. The 10.038 bore is J8 ±7 µm (Ø2.293–2.307 mm), against the 10.032 Ø2.3 h7 shaft (ISO current table 2.290–2.300 mm; drawing table gives 2.291–2.300 mm): a transition fit, up to 17 µm clearance and up to 7 µm interference (Derived), consistent with the sleeve's “tight sliding fit” note. The assembly's post-broaching instruction may still affect the final bore, so do not assign a finished assembly fit until that sequence is resolved. Radially, each 10.038 tooth envelope reaches Ø4.4–4.45 mm from the sleeve axis; no mating part or clearance is dimensioned on this sheet.

Project 3× reference

Per the project's tolerance strategy and project decisions, manufacturing tolerances are selected by process. The simple 3× tolerance is only a mathematical comparison; original general and non-ISO tolerances do not silently carry over. ISO grades, when used for a project design, are recalculated at the scaled basic size.
