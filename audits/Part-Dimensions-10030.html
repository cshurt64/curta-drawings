Scope and source status

  • Part: 10.030, Löscherkappenkörper (clearing-cap body), drawing 10.030-7, current; Ersatz für 10.030-6 (105.055), so it also replaces the old-series drawing 105.055. Identity agrees with Drawing-Index.md and the title block on drawing-set/catalog page 45 (viewer page 45). Scale 5:1 (reference view M1:1); stamped Abmasse in μ = 1/1000 mm.   • Material: Decoltal Ø42 (free-machining aluminium alloy, Ø42 mm bar stock). No surface or heat treatment stated.
  • General tolerance: ±100 µm linear, ±1° angular. Runout: 20 µm radial and side on marked surfaces, 50 µm on unmarked. Finish: ▽▽, with ▽▽▽ in brackets. Edges: Kantenbruch max. 0,1/45°. Dimensions marked ✱ (a filled circle or star on the sheet, glossed in the title block) are besonders kontrolliert (specially checked on acceptance).
  • Labels: printed content Confirmed; calculated values Derived; unresolved readings Unverified. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.
