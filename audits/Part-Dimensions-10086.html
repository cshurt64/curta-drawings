Derived checks (estimates, not sheet facts). (1) Mean coil diameter 1.9 − 0.2 = 1.7 mm = 2 × r0.85, so the printed radius and diameter agree. (2) 11 active coils × 0.44 mm = 4.84 mm, matching the drawn 4.85 mm maximum compression closely. (3) Sheet rate: (0.148 − 0.134) / (4.85 − 4.4) = 0.031 kg/mm (about 0.31 N/mm). (4) A textbook rate for this wire (Ø0.2, mean Ø1.7, 11 active coils, assumed E = 190 GPa) gives about 0.70 N/mm — roughly 2.3× the drawn rate, the same order of mismatch found on 10.073's spring. This recurring gap (about 2–2.5× across the two springs checked so far) suggests either a systematically different modulus for this wire than the assumed 190 GPa, or the idealised bending formula under-predicting flexibility for these small, closely-wound coils; it is not resolved from the drawings alone.
