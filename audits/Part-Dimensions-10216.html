  • Assembly fields: general linear tolerance ±50 µ; general angular tolerance blank; radial (*) and side runout ≤20 µ where marked, none stated for unmarked surfaces; predominant surface finish three triangles; material, surface treatment and heat treatment blank. Type I, group Grundkörper. Edges deburred and broken about 0.1 mm unless noted.
  • Labels: printed controls Confirmed; child-sheet dimensions Confirmed at their stated revisions; arithmetic Derived; unresolved readings Unverified; readings that rest on a judgment Inferred. 3× values are geometric references, not factory specifications.

No ISO fit code appears on this sheet: every limit is an explicit ± or +/− value, so no fit is assigned. ISO 286-1 defines the fit system and ISO 286-2 tabulates deviations; the child sheets' codes are in their own audits.

Derived stacks

Star (Derived). Body 4.35, so the Ø3 toothed head is 6.95 − 4.35 = 2.60 mm tall. If 6.1 is the head's top face, the teeth stand 6.95 − 6.1 = 0.85 mm above it (Inferred), and the 6.55 gauge height sits 0.40 mm below the tips and 0.45 mm above that face.

Axle inside the star (Derived, Inferred). The axle is 56 ±0.2 (10.014-7) and its end is 49.6 ±0.1 below the star's lower face, so about 56 − 49.6 = 6.4 mm of axle lies inside the star; added linearly, the tolerances make that 6.1–6.7 mm. That puts the axle end between the head's top face (6.1) and the gauge height (6.55), 0.25–0.85 mm below the tips. It assumes the star's lower face is the plane the 49.6 starts from, as the drawing shows.

Press-in (Derived under an unverified reading). A press-in travel of 0.4 mm on two 4 % tapers changes the diametral fit by about 0.4 × 0.04 = 0.016 mm if 4 % means 0.04 mm of diameter per mm of length; the percentage's definition is Unverified (Part-Dimensions-10014.md). The force of 70 kg is 70 × 9.80665 = 686 N if it is a kilogram-force, the usual reading (Inferred).

Relief (Derived). It starts 8.65 − 1.1 = 7.55 mm below the star's lower face and ends at 8.65 mm. Against the Ø2 h7 axle (Ø1.990–2.000) the Ø1.5 +50/0 relief is 0.22–0.25 mm deep per side (Derived). The relief is 6.4 + 7.55 = 13.95 mm below the embedded end of the axle and 49.6 − 8.65 = 40.95 mm above the free end (Derived). The 10.014-7 sheet records no such neck, so it is either cut after the star is pressed on or omitted from that sheet (Unverified). On the scan of 10.239 (see Part-Dimensions-10239.md) the neck is drawn from 7.5 to 8.6 mm, directly under the lower ring of the 10.222 sleeve stack (Derived, scan-measured).

Shaft length. 49.6 mm to the free end; the drawing is cut with break lines and is not to scale along the axle (Confirmed by the break marks).

The 50° tooth tolerance: a sign discrepancy (Unverified)

The tooth section reads 50° 0/−1° on this sheet (dated 2.6.1950, 400 dpi). The finished-star sheet 105.254 (dated 12.12.1950), at 500 dpi, reads 50° +1°/0 for the same flank; the earlier audit of that sheet read only the +40′/0 marks on the 45° measuring axis. Both are small print, so either sign may be misread, and the two sheets may genuinely differ. The band is 1° wide either way. The later sheet is the more likely to be current if they differ, but that is a guess (Unverified); the direction matters for a printed replica only in how the tooth flank is dimensioned, not for the 3× geometry.
