Relation to 10.020 and 10.017

This is the same sleeve as 10.020 (Ø2.7 h11 collar, Ø2 H8 bore, Ø2.3 h7 body, the same notch detail 0.61, 90° ±10′, r0.15, r0.05, centring ±20 µm, and the same note about a tight sliding fit with 10.038), with different lengths: 6.7 overall and 4.7 for the narrower part here, against 7.8 and 5.8 on 10.020. The 10.017 guide sleeve (Part-Dimensions-10017.md) has the same three diameters again with 4.7 and 2.7. Three sleeves of one diameter family differ only in length, one per setting-wheel variant (10.217, 10.218, 10.219).
